About

Where systems meet

Most of my work happens where systems meet: a screen and the service behind it, a calculation and the SQL that feeds it, an application and the identity, secrets and pipelines it depends on.

01

About & collaboration

I'm comfortable starting in an unfamiliar codebase. I read the business logic, map the moving parts, and write down what I find, whether that's a functional and technical write-up of a calculation process or a UML-style diagram of an API's object model.

I started out in NLP and machine learning at university, and I'm now steering my career back toward AI and data engineering. I bring habits from enterprise work with me: validate everything, reconcile the parts to the whole, and document how the numbers are derived.

How I work with teams

  • Explaining the work. Translating technical findings for business and engineering stakeholders, including demos to senior engineering and architecture leads.
  • Unblocking others. Helping developers with deployment setup, branch merging and GitHub workflows.
  • Handing over well. Knowledge transfer and written documentation, so fixes outlive the ticket.
  • Working across teams. Coordinating with platform and support teams on access, identity and environment changes.
