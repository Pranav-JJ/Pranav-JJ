          About & collaboration

          Most of my work happens where systems meet: a Vue screen and the C# service behind it, a calculation and the SQL that feeds it, an application and the identity, secrets and pipelines it depends on.

          I'm comfortable starting in an unfamiliar codebase. I read the business logic, map the moving parts, and write down what I find, whether that's a functional and technical write-up of a snapshot process or a UML-style diagram of an API's object model. I started out in NLP and machine learning at university, and these days that curiosity goes into practical, measured experiments with AI-assisted development.

          How I work with teams

          • Explaining the work. Translating technical findings for business and engineering stakeholders, including demos to senior engineering and AI-architecture leads.
          • Unblocking others. Helping developers with deployment setup, branch merging and GitHub workflows.
          • Handing over well. Knowledge transfer and written documentation, so fixes outlive the ticket.
          • Working across teams. Coordinating with platform and support teams on access, identity and environment changes.
